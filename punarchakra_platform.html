📖 About Us

PunarChakra (ಪುನಃಚಕ್ರ) is a technology‑driven circular‑waste platform based in the heart of Bengaluru, Karnataka. We connect households, businesses, and certified scrap collectors to turn everyday waste – newspapers 📰, electronics 💻, metals ⚙️, plastics 🥤, and more – into valuable resources, ensuring nothing ends up in landfills.
